# Naija Tax Guide - Tax Expert Review Benchmark

Purpose: create a repeatable expert-review pack for validating common Nigerian tax answers before stronger public launch, partner review, or startup committee presentation.

Reviewer role: Nigerian tax professional, accountant, tax consultant, or experienced tax administrator.

Review output for each answer:

- Pass: correct and safe enough for general guidance.
- Pass with edits: mostly correct, but needs wording, source, date, jurisdiction, or escalation edits.
- Fail: materially wrong, unsafe, overconfident, stale, or missing a critical warning.

Minimum fields to record:

- Question ID
- Product answer reviewed
- Correct answer summary
- Risk level: low / medium / high
- Source category: primary law / federal guidance / state practice / internal review / professional judgement
- Last reviewed date
- Reviewer name or initials
- Decision: pass / pass with edits / fail
- Notes and required change

## Review principles

1. Numeric claims, rates, thresholds, penalties, deadlines, and effective dates are high risk.
2. State-level personal income tax and PAYE answers should not imply every state process is identical.
3. Federal tax answers should not blur federal authority responsibilities with State Internal Revenue Service responsibilities.
4. AI answers should not claim to replace tax professionals, accountants, lawyers, FIRS/NRS, or state tax authorities.
5. Audits, disputes, penalties, tax notices, objections, appeals, litigation, back-duty assessments, restructuring, and cross-border tax questions should trigger professional escalation.
6. Answers should explain assumptions and ask for missing facts where needed.
7. Where an answer cannot be verified from available knowledge, it should say so instead of inventing law, rates, dates, or procedures.

## Recommended review cadence

- Review the first 100 benchmark answers before serious public launch.
- Re-review high-risk categories after major tax law changes, finance acts, official circulars, or platform prompt changes.
- Re-review any answer that generated a user complaint, refund request, or professional correction.

## Evidence to keep

Store reviewer comments, reviewed answer versions, and last-reviewed dates in a controlled location. Do not publish reviewer private notes directly unless approved. Public pages can safely state that a review process exists once the process is actually being followed.
